## 2. How to update your GitHub repository:

1. Open your repository on GitHub:
   👉 **[github.com/ahmedothman206/othconsultations.com](https://github.com/ahmedothman206/othconsultations.com)**

2. **Crucial First Step (Clear Custom Domain in Settings):**
   * Click **Settings** (gear icon at top).
   * Click **Pages** in the left menu.
   * Under **Custom domain**, make sure the box is **empty** (delete `othconsultations.com` if still there, and click **Save**).

3. **Upload the updated files:**
   * In your repository, click **Add file** → **Upload files**.
   * Drag and drop **`index.html`** and **`.nojekyll`**.
   * Click **Commit changes**.

4. **Visit your website:**
   Within 30–60 seconds, your site is live at:
   👉 **[https://ahmedothman206.github.io/othconsultations.com/](https://ahmedothman206.github.io/othconsultations.com/)**
